How to Use this Tool

1. Get a Free API Key: You need an API key to securely generate resumes in your browser. Choose a provider like Google Gemini, click the link to claim a free key, and paste it in the settings toggle above.

2. Paste Job Description: Copy the full text of the job you are applying to. This provides the AI keyword targets.

3. Paste Base Resume: Provide your current master resume. The AI will extract your experience and rewrite it to match the job requirements.

Note: All processing is done securely via the API. Your data is not stored on this portfolio website.
